Summary

On professional grounds, most of my time is spent writing. I'm a novel and short stories writer, and I maintain several blogs and newsletters.

On the personal side, I work on my garden to create a haven for bees and butterflies (and all the other creatures that are less attractive but still deserve to live), read a lot of books, try to be more consistent in my yoga and pilates practice, and in my study of Japanese, and I'm learning how to draw on a pen tablet. And many other things.

Priority project

Write my novel "Dwarves"

I decided to write a fantasy novel, which is very new to me. I'm currently finalizing my applications and will send my first emails to publishing houses soon.

Write short stories

I started writing short stories again to participate in writing contests. This is to enjoy short term projects (as opposed to writing novels, which takes six months to a year, not counting the time waiting for answers from publishers), but also in hope that I'd win some of them and earn a prize.

Sides

Those are projects that I'm building on the side when not busy with my priority project or when I need to put my mind at something else. They're either maintenance tasks, one-shots, or groundwork for future "priority projects".

Writing about my Japanese trip on The Flonicles

Why I'm doing this

It's a way for me and my partner to relive those moments, while sharing with people who want to organize their trip to Japan on their own. Since there's a lot of interest in Japan at the moment, it's a great time to post this. This is a long term project.

What I'm actually doing

  • Sorting pictures.
  • Writing posts in French and English.
  • Updating related posts with internal links and references.
  • Maybe updating part of previous posts content to avoid redundancy.

Learning how to draw book covers

Why I'm doing this

I'm thinking of self-publishing one of my novels and would love to design my own book cover. It's also a job I'd like to do so that I don't only rely on earnings linked to writing.

What I'm actually doing

  • Learning how to use my pen tablet.
  • Designing my book cover.
  • Drawing illustrations for one of my newsletters.

Personal curriculum

Here's what I'm learning at the moment, not only for professional purpose but also for personal growth.

  • Learning graphic design and editorial graphic work. I'd like to be able to create professional book layouts and draw book covers. This is a long term learning goal, which concretely includes: upskilling my hand drawing, learning how to draw with a pen tablet, watching tutorials and attending online courses in graphic design (general or book oriented). I might start creating zines about topics I learn about to practice those skills.
  • Learning Japanese: reading a book with anecdotes about food. One big step for my Japanese learning: I finally dared writing in my books!
