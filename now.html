Quitting GAFAMs

Why I'm doing this

I don't want to support GAFAMs and other non-ethical big tech companies. I want to move everything I can to services and compagnies that respect privacy, when possible located in Europe.

What I'm actually doing

  • Making the inventory of every service I'm using that belongs to unethical companies.
  • I'm moving my data and hosted services one step after the other.
  • Sharing about that journey on my Substack in hope it inspires others.
